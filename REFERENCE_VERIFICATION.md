# Reference verification list (version 11, 22 November 2026)

Each of the 78 references of version 11 is listed with the record against which its bibliographic details were checked on 22 November 2026 and with the result. The checks were made by a language model (Claude Fable 5.2, Anthropic) through the Crossref, Europe PMC and DataCite interfaces and on publisher or proceedings pages retrieved in the session. They cover authors, title, journal, year, volume, issue, pages and DOI.

What the checks do not cover: the model read abstracts or records, not every full text. Reading each cited passage against the full text remains an action for the author.

Summary: 74 references of version 10 checked; 18 entries corrected or completed; 4 references added and checked (numbers 42, 43, 71 and 72); every reference is cited in the manuscript and every citation has an entry.

DOIs. A DOI was added to 38 further entries from the Crossref record that matched the title, first author, volume and pages (numbers 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 19, 23, 24, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39, 41, 44, 46, 47, 50, 51, 52, 53, 56, 57, 70, 73, 74). Entries without a DOI: 48 (a book without a DOI); 55 (a journal that assigns no DOI); 64 (an article of 1987 for which two Crossref searches on 22 November 2026 returned no record).

Numbering: references 1 to 41 keep their numbers; former 42 to 67 moved up by 2; former 68 is 70; former 69 to 74 moved up by 4.

Claim support for the added references. Reference 42 studies tests for the existence of a non-negative solution of a linear system with known coefficients; the manuscript cites it for that question only. Reference 43 shows that standard inference for moment-inequality models can be spuriously precise when the model is misspecified and proposes inference for that case; the manuscript cites it for that description and states that SIGNET does not implement such inference. References 70 to 72 are cited for the Hartung-Knapp interval and its never-narrower modification.
